## Options
There are 4 options to choose from.
- Option 1(Intel 16"): Notebook ThinkPad P16s Gen 4 INTEL (U9)
- Option 2(Intel 14"): Notebook ThinkPad P14s Gen 6 INTEL (U9)
- Option 3(AMD 16"): Notebook ThinkPad P16s Gen 4 AMD (R9)
- Option 4(AMD 14"): Notebook ThinkPad P14s Gen6 AMD (R9)

Everyone pc has:
- 64 GiB RAM
- USB C charger+charging port
- 1TB SSD
- Integrated or brand-specific graphics card

The diff is as follows:
> [!NOTE]
>- AMD is cheaper and more often has better driver support than Intel
>- The Intel 16" option has 4K resolution, while the others have Full HD or 1920x1200 resolution.
